How to Generate Zoho Credentials in 2 Minutes

Follow these 3 simple steps to generate or rotate your permanent API credentials from the official Zoho API Console:

1

Open Zoho API Console

Visit api-console.zoho.in (or .com if outside India). Sign in with your Zoho account and click Add Client.

2

Select "Self Client" & Generate Code

Choose Self Client. Under Scope, paste: ZohoInventory.fullaccess.all,ZohoInventory.salesorders.all. Set Time Duration to 10 minutes and click Generate to get your authorization code.

3

Generate Permanent Refresh Token

Under the Client Secret tab, copy your Client ID and Client Secret. Use the authorization code in the box above to receive your permanent Refresh Token and save!

How to Add Products in Zoho Inventory

Once connected, any product you create in Zoho Inventory immediately appears on your storefront:

A

Create Item in Zoho

In your Zoho Inventory Dashboard, go to Items → click + New.

B

Fill in Product Details

• Item Name: Enter product title (e.g. Fourth Hokage Minato Inspired Oversized T-Shirt)
• SKU: Enter a unique identifier (e.g. TS-MIN-001)
• Selling Price: Enter price in ₹ INR (e.g. 1599)
• Initial Stock: Enter warehouse quantity on hand (e.g. 50)
• Upload Image: Attach product photo so it appears on the storefront.

C

Save & View on Storefront

Click Save in Zoho Inventory. Your store will automatically sync the catalog, render the item for your customers, and decrement stock when orders are placed!
